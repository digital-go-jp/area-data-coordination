# Area Data Coordination Platform
This repository is designed to provide access to various materials related to the "Area Data Coordination Platform" as outlined by the Digital Agency, along with standard data models and other relevant resources.


## Guidebook for Shared Use of the Area Data Coordination Platform
As the title suggests, this guide was originally created to develop a shared use vision for the Area Data Coordination Platform. It has since been revised to encompass not only the policies and considerations for shared platform utilization but also the Digital Agency's comprehensive perspective on the background, practical applications, and utilization of area data.

Published September 4, 2026 [Version 3](https://github.com/digital-go-jp/area-data-coordination/tree/main/guidebook)


## Standard Data Models for Area Data Utilization
These data models standardize frequently reused datasets at the regional level into reusable units, aiming to facilitate cross-referencing and mutual utilization of area data. The standardized data can be utilized without additional data conversion requirements in any scenario—whether for open data publication, inter-organizational data sharing, or service deployment using the Area Data Coordination Platform.

Published September 4, 2026 [First Edition](datamodels/Data Model for Area Data Utilization (Practical Guidelines).md)
